# What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, or **AI**, is technology designed to perform tasks that normally require abilities associated with human intelligence.

Examples include:

- recognizing pictures
- understanding speech
- translating languages
- predicting likely outcomes
- recommending videos
- generating stories
- controlling robots

## The most important idea

AI is not automatically a robot that thinks like a human.

Most AI is **specialized**. A chess system may be excellent at chess but unable to drive a car or cook dinner.

## The course journey

### 1. Rules

A human writes the rules.

```text
IF it is raining
THEN take an umbrella
```

### 2. Learning

Instead of writing every rule, we show the computer examples.

```text
examples → training → model → prediction
```

### 3. Neural Networks

A network of mathematical units learns complicated patterns.

```text
inputs → layers → output
```

### 4. Creating

Generative AI produces new text, pictures, music, code, or other content.

### 5. Reasoning

Modern AI systems can break a problem into steps, compare options, and use information to reach a result. Reasoning systems can still make mistakes.

### 6. Acting

An AI agent can receive a goal, select a tool, perform an action, check the result, and continue.

### 7. The Future

The future may include more capable multimodal systems, robots, safer agents, and continuing research into AGI.

## AI can make mistakes

Children should remember:

> A confident AI answer is not automatically a correct answer.

Always verify important information.
