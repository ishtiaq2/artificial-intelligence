- [ ] B) It was raining heavily that day, so no one went outside.

### Answer Key:
1. **C** (Past data and patterns)
2. **B** (The AI searches for mathematical patterns in information)
3. **A** (pandas)
4. **B** (matplotlib)
5. **C** (Comma-Separated Values)
6. **B** (It was raining heavily that day... This teaches students about "Outliers" and real-world data context!)
7. **False** (AI needs LOTS of data to find accurate patterns!)
