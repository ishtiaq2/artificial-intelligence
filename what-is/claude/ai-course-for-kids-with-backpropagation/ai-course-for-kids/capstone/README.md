# Capstone Project: Design Your Own AI System

**Time:** 60–90 minutes | **Format:** pairs or small groups | **Output:** a short presentation (2 min/group)

## The Task
Design a fictional AI product or system and answer the following, using `capstone_template.md` as a
worksheet:

1. **Which stop(s) on the journey does it use?** (Rules? Learning? Neural Networks? Creating?
   Reasoning? Acting? Would it eventually need AGI?)
2. **What data would it need to be trained on?**
3. **What could go wrong** if the training data were biased or incomplete?
4. **One safety rule** the group would build in before letting it act autonomously.
5. **Sketch or describe the sense-think-act loop** if it involves acting in the real world.

## Presentation
Each group presents for 2 minutes:
- Name and one-sentence pitch for the AI system
- Which journey stops it uses, and why
- Their answer to the Data & Bias Checkpoint
- Their one safety rule

## Rubric (100 pts)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Clarity of the idea | 25 |
| Correct use of AI vocabulary from the 7 modules | 25 |
| Data & bias awareness | 25 |
| Safety consideration | 25 |

## Why This Works
The capstone forces synthesis across all eight stops of the journey and surfaces AI ethics
naturally, rather than as a bolted-on lecture — closing the loop that started with Module 1's
"Whose rules are these?" question.
